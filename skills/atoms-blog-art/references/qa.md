# Visual review — evidence, not scores

Compare actual artwork with the abstract reference subset at matched display sizes. Name visible differences; do not claim parity from a rubric total.

- Hierarchy: one focal event; intentional cropping; no competing details.
- Finish: inspect edges, transitions and overlaps for banding, smearing, muddy blends, unnecessary glow, malformed geometry, heavy grain or stock gloss. Smooth must not become empty.
- Color: compare with token swatches. Natural shading is valid. Flag foreign hue families and excessive Iris domination. Sampling supports judgment, not taste.
- Meaning: link the relationship to the source in one plain sentence. Do not require literal icons or exhaustive technical explanation.
- Crop: inspect 16:9, central square, thumbnail and actual page with badge. The important relationship must remain.
- Series: review contact sheets in page order for repetition of ribbons/cards/circles and inconsistency of finish. Vary geometry, lightness and direction.
- Reality: verify selected files actually appear on the page. File, mapping, dimension, opacity, compression and layout checks do not measure artistic quality.

Record candidate, source/prompt, observed issue, action, result and remaining limitation. Accept/revise/reject with reasons. Failed pilots stay explicitly rejected. Independent reviewers inspect images before reading the generator's self-evaluation.
