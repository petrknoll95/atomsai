Useful combinations: cream / forest / Iris; forest shade / sage / Iris light; stone / deep green / cream. A starting hierarchy is 60% background, 30% dominant form, 10% accent; the image matters more than numerical exactness. Use only the colors the composition needs.

Separate warm family, used only with explicit rationale: Crimson #9A3324, Orange #FF671F, Apricot #FDBE87. Do not mix warm and Iris accents in one cover. Neutral brand colors exist but are not needed for this series.
