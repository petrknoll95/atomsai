# Centered star-field hero

The homepage now uses the star variant. The previous nebula/slit hero is preserved at `/hero-variants/nebula`, with its renderer and layout untouched. `src/components/home/home-page.tsx` selects either hero while sharing the existing header and page sections.

This variant centers the existing headline, description, and CTA. The supplied reference informs the scattered circular particles and depth, not the reference's image collage or search interface. The light page background and forest particles use the existing brand palette.

`starfield-hero/field.ts` implements a separate seeded 3D scene with 3,600 particles on desktop and 2,000 on mobile. Dots have a minimum screen diameter of 1.7 CSS pixels, perspective-dependent radius and opacity, and a slow forward drift. Hover pans the camera smoothly through the whole field without displacing individual dots. A soft quiet area behind the copy protects readability without putting an opaque panel over the field.

Hover picks one nearby dot as a stable hub. Eight to twelve irregular branches connect real dots across a 190–300px neighborhood, and most branches extend to one additional unused neighbor; there are no cross-links or third hops. Stable weighted sampling varies branch angles and lengths without evenly spaced directions or a shared radius. The shared origin and branch endpoints continuously ease toward new dots during handovers without dimming the network. New branches are matched to existing drawn positions, and the nearest eight neighbors remain eligible in sparse areas. Connected dots smoothly enlarge by up to 35% and settle back when released. Visibility is consistent across depth and the central text area. Connections fade away only on pointer exit and during the section approach; touch and reduced motion do not activate them. Browser checks sample every animation frame during pointer sweeps to verify uninterrupted connections through multiple hub changes.

The canvas renders at native device pixel density. Reduced motion is static; hidden/offscreen drawing pauses; touch listeners are passive. Scrolling moves the camera forward toward the next section: the cards appear at a smaller perspective scale, become fully opaque early, and grow smoothly into their final layout, followed by a brief hold. Both section wrappers are transparent so no moving background edge clips the particles. The field fades away as the cards approach, and particles fade near the camera rather than popping out. There is no destination particle or circular aperture. Responsive track lengths are defined in the variant CSS. The proven pin/reveal layout styles are shared with the saved hero.

Verification: `node scripts/starfield-hero-check.mjs` covers Chromium and WebKit at desktop and small-phone sizes, centered layout, native resolution, particle counts, camera pan and return, the continuous opaque reveal, pin release, reduced motion, and the saved nebula route. Screenshots and results are stored in this folder.
