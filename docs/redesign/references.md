# Section inspiration and design decisions

Mobbin searches use the same task intent and code destination. References were visually inspected, not selected from names alone. Adapt hierarchy and information design to the existing homepage; no copied product assets.

## Initial review

- [Tines feature section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/da5302c8-e58d-4d5f-b2f0-79089c5674bc): heading occupies one grid position; individual workflow illustrations explain the claims. Adopt the precision, avoid a site-wide bento pattern.
- [V7 feature section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ca187f4d-5cf3-415f-958f-aa8205c36f6d): compact UI evidence under each feature. Useful for concrete call and scheduling examples.
- [ClickUp platform section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/edaded65-c9b9-42df-8e5d-dc12d802c6e3): central illustration with peripheral categories. Too dense for this project; not carried into implementation.

## Contact research

- [Notion contact sales](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0e4208ae-5a28-41cf-9275-3f21977e394c): quiet two-column introduction and form, with little visual competition. Most appropriate hierarchy for AtomsAI.
- [Framer contact sales](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/914df681-c24d-4800-bd11-9c144d0175fb): form and value proposition occupy the same opening view. Retain proximity; reject dark gradient backdrop and additional qualification fields.
- [Miro contact form](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/270f478a-6a90-49fb-8b34-f5751d7254f9): long single-column qualification. AtomsAI needs fewer inputs and a warmer invitation.

## Trust research

- [Miro Trust Center](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e7f37059-a386-4dd1-b4e5-d41d830ad449): concise introduction and recognizable security/privacy/compliance categories. Convert the categories to a document-oriented page with direct section navigation.
- [Canva trust hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/573de197-4e3c-43c0-a53c-11a7494d46b1): restrained hierarchy and context navigation. AtomsAI should use an architectural diagram rather than a generic meeting photograph.
- [Maze security](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b07657a6-9166-45b2-bdc3-3fc8e29e4534): specific control descriptions but a vivid card grid. Keep the specificity; use quiet rows rather than equally weighted boxes.

Page-specific research and iterations are recorded in approach-design.md, voice-design.md, healthcare-design.md and subsequent contact/trust notes.
