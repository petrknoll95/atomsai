Does the agent negotiate or discuss offers?
No. It answers questions about listings and process from information you approve, and hands pricing, offers and contracts to your licensed agents.
Which CRMs and property systems does it work with?
We connect to the systems you run, such as Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Lofty, AppFolio, Yardi or Buildium, and to your calendars, email and phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
How does it treat fair housing?
Every lead gets the same information and options. The agent doesn’t steer or describe neighborhoods in ways your team hasn’t approved, and every conversation is logged.
Can it book showings into each agent’s calendar?
Yes. It books against real availability, sends confirmations and access details, and rebooks when plans change.
Is it secure?
AtomsAI comes from the team behind JustCall. It is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, and every conversation, booking and handover is logged.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
