Does the agent make credit decisions?
No. It answers from the product rules you approve, collects applications and documents, and hands decisions to your team.
Which loan systems does it work with?
We connect to the systems you run, such as Encompass, MeridianLink, LoanPro or nCino, and to your CRM, email and phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
Can it help with collections?
Yes, for early-stage reminders, promises to pay and payment plans your rules allow, inside your consent, quiet-hours and contact-frequency rules. Hardship cases go to your team.
Does it work on the phone as well as text and chat?
Yes. One agent works across web chat, phone, SMS and email with one memory, so a borrower who texts and then calls doesn’t start over.
Is it secure?
AtomsAI comes from the team behind JustCall. It is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, borrowers are verified before account details are shared, and every conversation is logged.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
