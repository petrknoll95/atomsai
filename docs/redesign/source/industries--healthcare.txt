Is the agent HIPAA compliant?
Yes. AtomsAI is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, patients are verified before anything about their care or bill is shared, and every conversation is logged.
Does it give medical advice?
No. It handles scheduling, intake and administrative questions. Clinical questions and anything urgent go to your clinical team, following the escalation rules you set.
Which practice systems does it work with?
We connect to the systems you run, such as athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, NextGen, Epic, Dentrix or Open Dental, and to your phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
Can it handle all our locations?
Yes. One agent answers for every location, with each site’s hours, providers and services, and one view of everything it handled.
Will patients know they’re talking to an AI agent?
You decide how the agent introduces itself. Patients can ask for a person at any time.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
