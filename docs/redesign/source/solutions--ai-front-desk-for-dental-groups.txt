A dental group’s patients don’t all call. They message the website chat at lunch, text after work, reply to a reminder, email about their child’s appointment, and call when they’re in pain. AtomsAI’s front desk agent answers every channel with one memory: it books into your practice software, confirms and reschedules, works your recall list, and hands anything clinical or financial to your team with a summary. What the work looks like today
Most front desks are built around the phone and the schedule. Practice software does that part well: it holds the calendar, the operatories, the providers and the patient record.
But patients now arrive from everywhere. The website chat goes to an email alias. Texts sit on someone’s phone. Reminder replies land in a texting tool. Calls stack up at 8am and at lunch, while the same two people are checking patients in and out. Each channel is handled in a different place, by whoever gets to it, and none of them writes back into the schedule on its own.
That’s the gap. Conversations on those channels don’t become appointments until someone types them in.
What the agent does
Each workflow below runs in your practice software.
New patients, on any channel, any time
Trigger:
a new patient asks about a first visit on the website chat at 9pm, or by text, or by phone.
Agent:
answers, asks what they need and which office suits them, checks for an existing record before creating one, collects insurance details for your team to verify, and books a new-patient exam with the right provider.
Result:
the patient is on tomorrow’s schedule, confirmed on the channel they used, with a note on the appointment saying what they asked.
Confirmations on the channel patients answer
Trigger:
an appointment two days out is still unconfirmed.
Agent:
replaces or works alongside the reminders you already send: a text, then an email if there’s no reply, and a call if your rules allow. It marks the appointment confirmed as soon as the patient answers, and offers new times if they can’t make it.
Result:
reschedules go straight back onto the schedule, and the front desk sees who confirmed without chasing anyone. See
why people no-show after a reminder
.
Filling a cancelled slot
Trigger:
a patient cancels tomorrow’s 9:00am hygiene appointment.
Agent:
records it as a cancelled appointment the way your office does, adds it to the unscheduled list if you want, then offers the slot to patients on your short-notice list and those due for recall who need that type of visit, on their preferred channel.
Result:
the first to accept gets the slot, and the rest are told it’s taken.
Recall that runs every week
Trigger:
patients past due for their cleaning.
Agent:
reaches out, answers questions from your approved answers, books them, and logs each contact on the patient’s record.
Result:
past-due patients are contacted every week without anyone building a list.
The same questions, answered once
Trigger:
“Where do I park?”, “Can I bring my child to the same visit?”, “What should I bring?”
Agent:
answers from your office’s approved answers, on whichever channel the patient used, and hands anything clinical, financial or insurance-related to your team with a summary.
Result:
fewer interruptions for routine questions.

What you control
Booking rules:
which appointment types the agent may book, for which providers and operatories, and how far ahead.
Channels and hours:
which channels it answers, and during which hours.
Your voice:
the messages it sends, in your practice’s voice.
Staged rollout:
new-patient bookings can go to a person for approval for the first few weeks, then run on their own once you trust them.
Pain and emergencies:
pain, swelling or injury goes straight to your on-call line or team, with the conversation attached. The agent never assesses symptoms.
Handover:
when a patient asks for a person, or the agent isn’t sure, it hands over with a summary, what it already tried, and the full conversation. Your team can pause it for one patient or step into any conversation.
Hard limits:
the agent never changes clinical notes or treatment plans, never posts payments and never deletes records.

Questions buyers ask
Can an AI agent book appointments straight into our practice software?
Yes. The agent checks open time for the right provider and operatory, books on the patient’s record and adds a note saying how it was booked.
Which practice software can it connect to?
We build the connection to your practice software as part of your build, through the route its maker provides (for ex. Open Dental’s API, the Dentrix Developer Program, Patterson’s partner program for Eaglesoft, or Curve Dental’s partner program), and confirm it in a front-desk workflow review before you commit.
Does it work for a group with several offices?
Yes. The agent knows which providers, operatories and hours each office has, so a patient who messages the main number or website is booked at the office they want.
Is patient data safe?
AtomsAI is SOC 2 Type II and we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) before we touch any patient data. The agent only reads and writes what its job needs.
How long does it take to go live?
A standard front desk agent goes live in days. Custom work across your practice software, phones and website takes 4 to 8 weeks.
