Can the agent replace our BDC?
It handles the work your BDC can’t get to, like nights, weekends and follow-up on every lead, and hands warm customers to your people. Most groups use it alongside their team.
Which CRMs and DMSs does it work with?
We connect to the systems you run, such as CDK Global, Reynolds and Reynolds, Tekion, VinSolutions, DealerSocket or Elead, and to your inventory and phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
Will it quote prices or financing?
Only the prices and offers your managers approve. Negotiation, trade values and financing terms go to your team.
Does it answer service calls too?
Yes. One agent works across sales and service, on chat, phone, SMS and email, with one memory per customer.
Is it secure?
AtomsAI comes from the team behind JustCall. It is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, and every conversation, booking and handover is logged.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
