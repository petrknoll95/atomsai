Read the Trust Center
What executives ask before signing.
01
What do you need from us, realistically?
Two to four practitioners for the teardown sessions, a process owner who can make decisions, an engineering contact for access, and a security reviewer.
Access provisioning can be the main dependency — read-only access for discovery, write scopes before the shadow lane. We identify approval and service-account requirements in the blueprint so access does not become a surprise during the build.
02
How is this different from hiring a consultancy or an agency?
We do the consulting part — the teardown, the baseline, the sequencing call — and then we build the thing. The deliverable is a running system in your repository, not a recommendation deck. The people in the teardown are the people who write the code. There is no discovery phase that bills for six weeks, and no hand-off to a delivery team you haven’t met.
If it helps: our first output is a ranked list that sometimes tells you not to buy anything yet.
Consultants who ship.
03
What if the workflow turns out not to be worth automating?
Then the teardown said so and you saved a quarter. We baseline before we build, and if the numbers don’t clear a payback bar we’ll say it out loud and point at the next workflow on the list.
We’d rather lose one engagement than ship something your team quietly abandons in month three.
04
Who owns the code, the prompts and the evals?
You do, from the first merge. Code, prompts, pipelines, eval suites, infrastructure definitions and runbooks land in your repository under your license as we go. There is no runtime you rent from us and no component that stops working if you stop working with us.
This is your moat — not ours.
05
Can you work inside our security and compliance constraints?
That’s the normal case. Your cloud account, your VPC, your KMS keys, your model provider, including self-hosted. SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA-capable. RBAC per action, approval gates, queryable audit trail, and a contractual commitment not to train on your data.
The blueprint contains the data-flow diagram your security team needs, and we’ll fill in your questionnaire.
typical security review turnaround
06
What happens after handover?
Your team operates it with us alongside them until the handover is agreed, then it’s yours. Some clients bring us back per workflow, some move to an embedded pod, some never call again — all three are fine outcomes.
What we won’t do is make continued payment a condition of the system continuing to run.
