Which TMS and tracking systems does it work with?
We connect to the systems you run, such as MercuryGate, Oracle TMS, Descartes, project44 or Samsara, and to your finance system, email and phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
Does the agent quote rates or approve claims?
No. It answers from your tracking and approved information, collects what your team needs, and hands rates, credits and claims to your people.
Can it answer the phone as well as text?
Yes. One agent works across web chat, phone, SMS and email with one memory, so a customer who texts and then calls doesn’t start over.
How does it handle proof of delivery?
It requests the signed POD, checks it against the bill of lading, and queues the invoice in your finance system. Anything that doesn’t match goes to your team.
Is it secure?
AtomsAI comes from the team behind JustCall. It is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, and every conversation, document and handover is logged.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
