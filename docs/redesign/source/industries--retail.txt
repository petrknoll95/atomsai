Does the agent recommend from our real catalogue?
Yes. It reads your live catalogue, stock and prices, and recommends only what you can sell. It never invents a product or a discount.
Which commerce and support systems does it work with?
We connect to the systems you run, such as Shopify, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, NetSuite, Klaviyo or Zendesk, and to your carriers and phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
Can it handle returns and exchanges?
Yes, following your return policy exactly. Anything outside the policy goes to your team with the details.
Does it work for our stores as well as online?
Yes. One agent answers your website, store phones, SMS and email, with each store’s hours and stock.
Is it secure?
AtomsAI comes from the team behind JustCall. It is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, and every conversation is logged.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
