Which ERPs does it work with?
We connect to the systems you run, such as SAP, Epicor, NetSuite or Infor, and to your CRM, quoting tool, email and phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
Does the agent set prices or approve quotes?
No. It drafts quotes from your price lists and ERP, and a person approves before anything is sent. Custom parts and discounts always go to your team.
Can it read drawings and RFQ attachments?
It reads RFQ emails and attached documents to list parts, quantities and dates. Anything it can’t match to a part number goes to your team for review.
Does it work for distributors and dealers as well as end customers?
Yes. One agent answers every account on chat, phone, SMS and email, with each account’s own pricing and history.
Is it secure?
AtomsAI comes from the team behind JustCall. It is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, and every conversation, quote and claim is logged.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
