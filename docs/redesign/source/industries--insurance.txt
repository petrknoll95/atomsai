Does the agent give coverage advice or bind policies?
No. It answers from the information your team approves, collects what your producers need, and hands anything that needs a licensed person to your team with the full conversation.
Which agency management systems can it connect to?
We connect to the system you run, such as Applied Epic, AMS360, HawkSoft or EZLynx, and to your CRM, email and phones. The connections are built as part of your build.
Can it answer the phone as well as chat?
Yes. One agent works across web chat, phone, SMS and email with one memory, so a client who chats at night and calls in the morning doesn’t repeat themselves.
How does it handle texting rules?
Texts and calls go only to people who have given consent, inside the quiet hours you set, and every opt-out is honored straight away.
Is it secure?
AtomsAI comes from the team behind JustCall. It is SOC 2 Type II, signs a BAA for HIPAA, and follows GDPR.
How long does it take to go live?
It depends on the workflows and the systems we connect. In the demo we look at yours and agree what the first build covers.
