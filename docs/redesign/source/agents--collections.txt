Why it matters
When customers fall behind, most of them want to pay.
They forgot, they can't pay it all at once, or they'd rather not take a call about it.
Your team can only reach a few of them in time.
Too many accounts to call
Teams reach the biggest balances first. Smaller ones drift further behind until they're written off.
One wrong call is costly
Calling at the wrong time, too often, or without the right disclosures brings complaints and fines.
Customers avoid the call
Many people won't pick up a collections call, but will answer a text and pay by link.
Most people want to pay.
They just need a nudge in time.
