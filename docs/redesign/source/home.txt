Turn operational friction into operating leverage.
The biggest constraint on growth is often hidden inside everyday work. Information copied between systems. Approvals chased manually. Documents reviewed twice. Exceptions sitting in queues. Experienced people spending their time keeping broken workflows moving.
Each workaround looks small. Together, they determine how fast your business can grow — and what that growth costs. AtomsAI redesigns these workflows around connected intelligence.
We combine AI, integration and human judgment to help work move across your business with greater speed, accuracy and control. We build inside your existing technology stack, around your operating rules and against measurable business outcomes. Your people spend less time carrying work between systems. Your technology completes more of the process. And your operating capacity grows before your cost base does.

The five questions you’ll ask anyway.
01
How do you handle our security and data-residency requirements?
The system runs where you tell it to: your VPC, your cloud account, your KMS keys, your choice of model provider. We are SOC 2 Type II and support HIPAA workloads. We don’t train on your data — that’s contractual, not a toggle.
Every agent action is permissioned, logged and reversible, and the audit trail is queryable by your team. Full detail, deployment matrix and documents live in the Trust Center.
02
How long until something is actually in production?
First workflow typically reaches production in 4–8 weeks. Teardown is 30 minutes to two days. Blueprint is a week. Build and integration is two to five weeks depending on how many systems we write to. Evals and hardening add one to two.
If your access approvals take six weeks, so do we — that’s usually the critical path, and we’ll say so on day one.
03
Who owns what we build?
You do. Code, prompts, pipelines, eval suites, infrastructure definitions and runbooks land in your repository under your license, as we go — not at the end. There is no runtime you rent from us and nothing that stops working if you stop working with us.
This is your moat — not ours.
04
How do we know it paid for itself?
We baseline before we build: current cycle time, cost per unit of work, error and exception rate, volume. The blueprint states the target and the measurement method, and the eval scoreboard reports accuracy, cost and latency per workflow after launch.
If a workflow can’t be measured, we tell you before the contract, not after.
05
Do you know our industry?
We’ve delivered in healthcare, insurance, financial services, logistics and manufacturing. But the teardown assumes we know nothing: we watch your people do the work before we propose anything.
Domain depth matters less than the willingness to sit with the exception cases for two days.
