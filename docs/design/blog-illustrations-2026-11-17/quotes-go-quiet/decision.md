# The quote went out. Then nothing.

Selected concept: an unmistakable open envelope with a document partly emerging. The quote is visibly still waiting for a reply. The document is the single highlighted part. This replaces the rejected abstract joined-sheet concept, retained in `recipe.abstract-rejected.mjs`.

Source: https://atomsai.com/resources/blog/quotes-go-quiet; the local imported body describes an emailed quote followed by silence and disconnected follow-up channels. No article text was changed.

Geometry: a 140 × 80 envelope pocket has 16 units of true depth, five physical faces and an open mouth. A triangular back flap folds away from the mouth along a shared hinge. Front fold seams meet at one point. A 106-unit-wide rectangular document sits inside the pocket and extends above it. The focal fade covers only its exposed front face. No text, decorative connector or extra object is used.

Validation: all eight Dark/Light neutral, oblique, back and underside exports passed the current skill renderer checks. Viewed the full contact sheet. The pocket reads as an envelope in the neutral and oblique views, its side and bottom preserve depth, and the document stays correctly behind the front and ahead of the back flap. The hidden front highlight disappears on the back view. No hanging joints, duplicate surfaces or impossible crossing were visible. Recipe checks verify an open mouth, a document that fits in the depth and width, orthogonal sheet dimensions and a flap attached to the actual rear mouth edge.

Parent integration preserves the approved v2 fields, Paper outlines, Cream highlight, SVG and 4:3 placement, and applies the requested thinner line weight. Review files keep the skill's diagnostic palette. Selected geometric master: `review/neutral-dark.svg`.
