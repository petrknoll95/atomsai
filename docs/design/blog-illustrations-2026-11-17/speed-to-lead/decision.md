# The homeowner asked three companies at lunch

Selected concept: a recognizable gabled house receives one message directly through a short curved connection. The message is the single highlighted part. The house identifies the homeowner immediately; the direct arrival expresses a useful reply while the customer is still deciding. This replaces the rejected racing spheres and earlier cubes, retained in `recipe.race-rejected.mjs` and `recipe.cubes-rejected.mjs`.

Source: https://atomsai.com/resources/blog/speed-to-lead; the local imported article describes leads from several channels and the first useful response winning the homeowner's business.

Geometry: one complete pentagonal house prism, with matching front and back gables, equal roof slopes, two side walls and a floor. A shallow door panel is physically attached to the front wall at floor level. A 52 × 36 message envelope has 5 units of real depth and a structural flap seam on its front face. Its connector begins on the envelope corner and ends with an in-plane arrow exactly at the receiving house wall. Nothing floats as an unrelated decoration.

Validation: all eight Dark/Light neutral, oblique, back and underside exports passed and the contact sheet was inspected. The house and envelope read clearly in neutral and oblique views. Back and underside show coherent actual solids, a correctly attached door and roof, and the same connector; the front envelope highlight disappears when its face turns away. No exposed triangulation, duplicate roof seams, detached connection or impossible joint was visible. Recipe assertions verify a closed house shell with exactly two faces sharing every edge, a centered roof ridge, positive depth, floor-aligned attached door, message separation, and exact connector endpoints.
